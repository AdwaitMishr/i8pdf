* Say the numbers as words, slowly. "Eighty-one thousand four hundred and
  fifteen million" against "eight thousand one hundred and forty-two crore" is
  the single most important moment in the video. Let both evidence panes sit on
  screen while you say it.

* The strongest half minute is 1:24 - 1:50, Case 3. If you overrun, cut from
  the architecture section, never from there.

* Do not apologise for Case 2 or Case 4. "No contradiction survived the context
  checks" is a finding, and naming your own failure modes reads as confidence.

* If a filter is slow, keep talking. The explanation carries the point; the card
  only illustrates it.

* Cuts, in the order to make them if you are running over 3:00:
    1. "And that caption sat in a different cell" in Case 1        -5s
    2. "Apparent contradictions that context explains." in Case 3  -3s
    3. the whole live-upload section at 0:46                       -14s

* One thing worth saying if a question comes up afterwards, but not on camera:
  the two probe notes in data/probe are synthetic, generated by
  scripts/make_probe_pdf.py, and each says so on its own first line. They exist
  only because the real corpus contains no surviving contradiction.
